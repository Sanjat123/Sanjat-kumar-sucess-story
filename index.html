How it started

I didn't wait for a placement season to start building. Somewhere in the middle of my BCA, I realized the fastest way to actually learn software engineering wasn't another course — it was shipping something real, for someone who'd actually use it and pay for it. So that's what I did.

What began as freelance contract work turned into something closer to founding a small studio. I took on client engagements end-to-end — architecture, APIs, database design, deployment — and led a small team of engineers through them using proper Agile sprints, not just vibes. Along the way I signed three enterprise contracts, published four apps to the Play Store, and built systems that now run in production for thousands of real users, not test accounts.

One of my proudest engagements has been the Universal CRM Platform, a multi-tenant SaaS system I built for Maa Enterprises — formalized through an official Chandigarh University office order, under the mentorship and supervision of my HOD, Dr. Arun Kumar Singh.
